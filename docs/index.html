The inertia challenge

A scoreboard for k-means. A submission is the label of each sample of a dataset, and its score is the inertia of these labels: the sum of the squared distances of the samples to the mean of their cluster. Lower is better.

A score is listed when it is less than 5% above the best one at the time it is submitted. The time, the hardware and the tags are what the user says about the submission: they are not checked. The datasets and the scoring are in the repository.
